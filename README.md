# Physics-Constrained Inverse Metallurgical Optimization Engine

An advanced, data-driven computational framework designed to approach critical mineral separation and high-value gemstone recovery as a native multi-variable topological optimization problem rather than a traditional chemical guess.

## 🚀 The Core Objective
Traditional mineral processing relies on 12 to 18 months of highly iterative, empirical laboratory "forward" testing (adjusting pH, temperature, and chemicals blindly to see what happens). This engine **flips the mathematical equation via Inverse Optimization**. By evaluating the multidimensional physical, thermodynamic, and spectral signatures of raw ores, the neural network models the exact path of least resistance required to selectively liberate target materials, compressing R&D cycles down from months to weeks.

## 🛠️ System Architecture & Features
*   **Inverse Optimization Logic:** Predicts optimal operational parameters natively from the structural state of the ore.
*   **Multi-Elemental Targeting:** Tailored to isolate complex Copper (Cu), Molybdenum (Mo), and heavy Lanthanide/Rare Earth Element (REE) suites.
*   **High-Value Gemstone Isolation Module:** Runs parallel real-time Hyperspectral Imaging (HSI) and X-ray Transmission (XRT) telemetry scans to detect and pneumatically bypass ultra-value mineral lattices (e.g., Painite, Red Beryl) before destructive milling fractures the crystal structures.

## 📊 How the Brain Computes (The Physics)
Instead of relying on broad chemical database approximations, the core engine treats mineral matrices as static geometric cages. The optimization loop evaluates incoming particle characteristics—such as peak reflectance wavebands (nm) and spatial densities ($g/cm^3$)—and cross-references them against known solid-state physics constants to determine selective separation actions instantly.
